The Supply Chain Dilemma & Problem Statement
Why traditional retail supply chains experience fragile coordination

Modern retail supply networks suffer from functional silos, where demand forecasting, inventory management, procurement, logistics, and financial capital allocation operate with isolated objectives:

  • Bullwhip Effect & Forecast Disconnect: Small demand variations at store checkouts create amplified swings upstream, leading to either stockouts or bloated safety stock.
  • Conflicting Agent Goals: Inventory planners optimize for 100% service level; Finance mandates strict working capital limits; Logistics pushes for full-truckload bulk shipments regardless of store holding capacity.
  • Supplier Volatility Blind Spots: Lead time delays (e.g. Gamma Imports at $\sigma=2.1\text{d}$) and promotional demand surges trigger sudden stockouts without advance warning.
  • Latency in Exception Handling: Manual spreadsheet coordination takes 3–5 days to react to supply disruptions, causing lost sales and costly rush expediting.
Objective: Build an integrated, autonomous multi-agent AI Control Tower that reconciles forecasts, dynamic safety buffers, and procurement constraints in real time with human-in-the-loop governance.
Implemented Solution: Symphony Engine
End-to-End AI Multi-Agent Coordination Framework

Symphony replaces fragmented manual workflows with a coordinated federation of 6 specialized AI agents:

  • Demand Agent: Machine learning ensemble forecast delivering 89.5% accuracy (10.5% MAPE) with $P_{10}/P_{50}/P_{90}$ quantile uncertainty envelopes.
  • Inventory Agent: Multi-echelon King's formula safety stock sizing accounting for both demand variance and supplier lead-time variance.
  • Procurement Agent: Automated purchase order generation adhering to vendor MOQs, lead times, and unit pricing tiers.
  • Logistics & Risk Agents: Real-time carrier volatility tracking, OTIF scoring, and disruption early-warning signals.
  • Orchestrator Agent: Automated mathematical consensus engine that resolves trade-offs between service levels and working capital ceilings.
Key Achievement: Maintained 100% Service Level (0 stockouts) across 126K daily records while reducing inventory carrying costs by $2.4M under an active $1.42M working capital budget.

Agent Roles, Algorithms & Architecture Design

Detailed specification of the 6 specialized AI agents, coordination algorithms, and conflict-resolution mechanisms

Federated Agentic Control Architecture
Symphony decomposes the retail supply chain into six autonomous yet coordinated micro-agents. Each agent runs specialized mathematical models and optimization routines tailored to its operational domain, while an Orchestrator Agent arbitrates inter-agent trade-offs (e.g. Service Level vs. Capital constraints) using a Weighted Multi-Objective Consensus Protocol.
DEM-01 Demand Agent
Ensemble Time-Series Forecasting & Driver Attribution
Core Objective: Generate accurate point and probabilistic quantile forecasts ($P_{10}$, $P_{50}$, $P_{90}$) across 70 SKUs and 10 stores.
Algorithms Implemented:
  • LightGBM & XGBoost gradient boosting ensemble with Prophet calendar regressors.
  • Multi-quantile Pinball Loss optimization for asymmetric inventory risk.
  • Promotional lift decomposition with halo & cannibalization cross-elasticity.
Inputs: Historical store sales (126K rows), price promotions, holiday calendars, store format scale factors.
Outputs: Weekly store-SKU demand distribution vectors → published to Inventory Agent & Risk Agent.
Performance: 89.5% Accuracy, 10.5% MAPE (vs 18.2% baseline naive model), +0.22% minimal bias.
INV-02 Inventory Optimization Agent
Dynamic Buffer Sizing & Multi-Echelon Stock Policies
Core Objective: Balance working capital expenditure against stockout probability to sustain 99%+ service levels.
Algorithms Implemented:
  • King's Dual-Variance Safety Stock Formulation:
    SS = z × √(μ_L · σ_D² + μ_D² · σ_L²)
  • Dynamic Reorder Point ($ROP = \mu_D \times \mu_L + SS$) dynamically tuned to lead-time variations.
  • ABC-XYZ classification for adaptive review cycle scheduling (continuous vs. periodic).
Inputs: Forecast distributions ($\mu_D, \sigma_D$), current stock on-hand, lead time estimates ($\mu_L, \sigma_L$).
Outputs: Net replenishment demand ($ROQ$), Days of Cover (DoC) → transmitted to Procurement Agent.
Performance: 100% Service Level (0 stockouts), $11.43M total inventory cost, 43.29× annual inventory turnover.
PROC-03 Procurement & Order Sizing Agent
Vendor Order Allocation, MOQ Optimization & PO Generation
Core Objective: Generate optimal Purchase Orders satisfying supplier MOQs, price breaks, and vendor split rules.
Algorithms Implemented:
  • Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) for multi-supplier order allocation.
  • Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) with quantity discount boundaries.
  • Dual-sourcing split heuristics (allocating volume between low-cost vs. high-reliability vendors).
Inputs: Net replenishment requests, vendor MOQs, tier unit prices, supplier lead-time constraints.
Outputs: Draft Purchase Orders, spending forecasts → transmitted to Logistics, Finance & Orchestrator.
Performance: 7 Actionable PO proposals actively staged with human-in-the-loop validation.
LOG-04 Logistics & Carrier Performance Agent
Transit Variability Monitoring, OTIF Tracking & Freight Optimization
Core Objective: Monitor carrier lead-time performance, predict delivery delays, and consolidate shipments.
Algorithms Implemented:
  • Empirical Lead-Time Distribution fitting (Kernel Density Estimation & Log-Normal distribution).
  • Carrier On-Time In-Full (OTIF) dynamic scorecards with rolling 30-day degradation alerts.
  • Container / Full-Truckload (FTL) cubic volume consolidation heuristics.
Inputs: ASN (Advance Shipping Notices), carrier transit logs, store receiving dock schedules.
Outputs: Lead-time parameter updates ($\mu_L, \sigma_L$) → fed back into Inventory and Risk Agents.
Performance: 93.2% Blended OTIF across 5 suppliers; isolated Gamma Imports variance ($\sigma=2.1\text{d}$).
RISK-05 Risk & Disruption Agent
Early-Warning Indicator Detection, Shock Simulation & Stress Testing
Core Objective: Detect early warning signals of supplier insolvency, port congestion, or severe demand surges.
Algorithms Implemented:
  • Monte Carlo simulation engine (1,000 iterations per scenario shock).
  • Multi-factor supplier vulnerability index: $Risk = f(\sigma_L, 1-OTIF, VolumeShare)$.
  • Dynamic shock escalation protocol triggering preemptive buffer reallocations.
Inputs: External supplier risk feeds, macro indicators, carrier bottleneck telemetry.
Outputs: Risk severity alerts, scenario stress curves → sent to Orchestrator and Scenario Sandbox.
Performance: Successfully flags high-risk supplier Gamma Imports and pre-simulates $+50\%$ lead time shocks.
ORCH-00 Master Orchestrator & Consensus Agent
Multi-Objective Arbitration, Constraint Enforcement & HITL Gatekeeper
Core Objective: Resolve cross-functional goal conflicts between agents and enforce enterprise constraints.
Algorithms Implemented:
  • Pareto-optimal trade-off frontier calculation across Service Level, Working Capital, and Logistics Cost.
  • Consensus Voting Matrix with weighted domain authority (Finance holds veto on working capital ceilings).
  • Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) confidence threshold gating: automated execution if confidence > 95%; planner approval required if ≤ 95%.
Inputs: Proposals, risk alerts, and operational requests from all 5 specialized agents.
Outputs: Final harmonized execution orders, dashboard consensus status, and planner recommendation queue.
Performance: Sub-second conflict resolution across cycle #848, reconciling $1.42M capital utilization.
